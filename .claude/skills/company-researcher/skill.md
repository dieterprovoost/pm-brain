Search the web for the company and collect useful business insights for us as a digital agency in order to grasp the context we are expected to work for.

If unclear which company we are looking for, ask for its head office location or VAT number.

We are looking for company size, composition of management, turnover figures. Look for investor relations information to obtain the latest, correct information.

List public customer touchpoints like websites or portals you can find locally and internationally. Checks recent activity on socials to obtain relevant news from the past year.

Create an "executive summary"-like overview of all useful information you can find which should allow us to understand the business the company operates in.
